# 0011: Open cluster 4/5/4 (configurable; 3/4/3 tried)

> **Superseded by [0013](0013-aligned-grid.md)** (aligned grid, 3/5/3 and VR 3/3/3). Return to this hex layout with
> `--dome-grid-offset: 1`, `--dome-cluster-middle: 5`, `--dome-cluster-outer: 4`, `--dome-banner-span: 2` (VR: 3/2/2).

> **Current: 4/5/4 on desktop, 2/3/2 in VR comfort mode** (`--dome-cluster-middle: 5`, VR override `3` — fewer,
> larger targets near the gaze in the headset; owner, 2026-10-05). 3/4/3 was tried first; the owner wants the banner in the top
> row, which 3/4/3 cannot do symmetrically (its top row has 3 cells centered on the gaze, a two-cell banner cannot
> be centered there). With 4/5/4 the gaze rests on a card again and the top row has 4 cells, so the two-cell
> banner sits centered in the top row with one card on each side. Notes on 3/4/3 below are kept for history.

Owner: "let's try showing the active cards as 3/4/3" — an experiment; 2/3/2 stays one token away.

## Decision

- `--dome-cluster-middle`: number of cards in the middle row of the open cluster; the rows above and below get one
  fewer. `4` → **3/4/3** (current), `3` → 2/3/2.
- On the hex lattice an even middle count puts the gaze **between two cards** (`restShift` 0.5): resting positions
  sit half a column off the cells of their row; rows above and below have a card straight above/below the gaze.
  `nearestCell`, `clusterAround`, `ringOf` and `homeRest` in `geometry.js` handle both counts.
- **Home** (3/4/3): the featured banner is centered in the middle row — its two cells are the two middle ones,
  with a card on each side (row -1, column 0).
- **Click to center**: a card becomes one of the two middle cards, on the side nearer the current view; the
  banner comes to the middle row. Arrow keys, joystick steps (incl. diagonals) and snapping keep the half-column
  rest offset, so movement stays straight.
- The cluster is wider than the 100° view, so the layout fits **width too** (`--dome-cluster-width-share` 0.94).
- Screen readers announce the left of the two middle cards as "centered"; the overlay pool grew to 30.

## Consequences

- 4/5/4: 13 open cards. At 1920×1080 cards are 303–385 px (center ≈ 311 px), the cluster spans x 58–1862 and does
  not touch the joystick; at 800×450 the joystick overlaps the bottom-right open card (the root size is clamped
  there, so the joystick is relatively large) — acceptable for now.
- 3/4/3 (history): 10 open cards instead of 7. At 1920×1080: cards 379–443 px (middle ≈ 395 px vs 422 px with 2/3/2);
  the cluster spans x 85–1835, y 178–1077.
