# Source: deovr.com homepage snapshot (2026-10-05)

Baseline of the current production homepage. Raw notes: [deovr-homepage-2026-10-05.md](../../raw/sites/deovr-homepage-2026-10-05.md).

## Key facts

- The live site uses a **light theme**; the OG / social image is already dark (near-black + gradient mark).
- Positioning line: up to **8K, 120 FPS**, **180° / 360° / 3D**, "every major VR headset".
- Discovery entry points: feed tabs (For You / New / Trending + period), banner carousel, Top Picks,
  Categories grid (8 categories with icons), Trending This Month.
- Card anatomy: thumbnail, duration, title, channel + avatar, age, views, actions (Add to Playlist,
  **Play in VR**, Hide), badges (Top Picks, Premium).
- Product features with their own gradient identity: Interactive, Passthrough, Script, VR Cams.

## Observations (baseline UX gaps, to be validated)

- Cards do not surface VR-specific metadata (resolution, FOV 180/360, stereo, FPS) at a glance (unverified — check logged-in and per-card views).
- "Play in VR" is one of several equal-weight actions; for headset users it is the primary action.
- Many top-level nav items (8) compete; headset users with ray input pay a higher cost per mis-click.
