# DeoVR

VR video streaming platform and player: a web catalog at [deovr.com](https://deovr.com/) plus native
apps for headsets. The subject of this redesign concept.

## Facts

- Content: 180°, 360° and 3D (stereo) VR video, up to 8K and 120 FPS ([raw](../../raw/sites/deovr-homepage-2026-10-05.md)).
- Claims support for "every major VR headset" ([deovr.com meta description](https://deovr.com/)).
- Sections: Videos, Photos, Premium Content, Channels, Passthrough, DriveAI, Categories, Playlists.
- Feature lines with their own color identity: Interactive, Passthrough, Script, VR Cams
  ([tokens](../../raw/brand/deovr-css-tokens-2026-10-05.txt)).
- Creator-facing: Upload, Creator Handbook, Developer Documentation, Brand Assets.

## Implications for the prototype

- Two audiences on the same URL: **desktop browsers** (browse, curate, then send to headset) and
  **headset browsers** (browse and play immediately). See [vr-design-principles](../synthesis/vr-design-principles.md).
- "Play in VR" is the conversion action of the catalog; the homepage should make it the hero action in headsets.
- The current light UI is the baseline; the concept explores dark mode ([brand-identity](../concepts/brand-identity.md)).
